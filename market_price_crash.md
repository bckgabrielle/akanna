# Sudden Market Price Crash

Keywords: price crash, low prices, cannot sell, market glut, buyers not paying, income shock

## Quick checks
Find out if the drop is local, one market or one buyer, or regional, by checking with farmers nearby. The right response is different for each.
Check whether the crop is perishable and needs to move soon, or storable, like grain or some tubers. That decides how much room you have to wait.

## What to do this week
For perishables you cannot sell fairly, prioritize eating it at home, simple drying or preservation, or trading locally rather than dumping it.
For storable crops, if safe storage is available, holding back part of it for a few weeks can help you avoid selling into the worst of a glut, if you can afford the wait.
Pool produce with neighboring farmers to negotiate together. Individual small sellers usually get the worst prices during a glut.
Look for buyers outside the usual market, schools, local institutions, or processors who might not be hit by the same price drop.
Talk to a local farmer cooperative or extension officer before taking on new debt to cover the gap. The terms matter as much as the amount.

## When to get help
A price crash that covers a whole region, or lasts more than one season, is worth a conversation about diversifying crops or trying cooperative marketing before the next planting decision.
